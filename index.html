About

Civil engineer with an MSc in earthquake-resistant structures and a PhD in topology optimization for composite material design from the National Technical University of Athens. Since 2018 I have worked on nine research projects in structural optimization, machine learning and additive manufacturing, and I have co-authored 17 publications.
